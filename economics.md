I have been interested in economics quite some time. In here, I intend to share the stuff I read, implement and experiment, in the field of economics.

1. [Small Businesses: An Introduction](https://www.agautham.io/strategy/2026/04/08/on-small-businesses.html): This is an introductory post on small businesses, from both a business and economics point of view.
2. [Productivity in Small Businesses: A note](https://www.agautham.io/strategy/2026/05/10/productivity-in-small-firms.html): This is an extension of the previous article, where I discuss the productivity of small businesses.
3. [Analysis of the International Trade Network with Community Core Detection](/economics/2026/07/12/analysis-of-itn-with-community-detection.html): Recently dabbling with graph partitioning/community detection algorithms and came across a very interesting use-case in international trade, which is the analysis of the international trade network (ITN) using such algorithms. The ITN can be viewed as one complex dynamic graph/network and interesting inferences can be made when it is studied across time. Although there are many cool papers on this topic, I picked up [this](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.6950) paper and implemented it. Got an opportunity to learn quite a bit in terms of working with real-world economics datasets (the CEPII-BACI dataset is used in the paper), trying to understand a phenomenon that happened in the world economy using certain algorithms.
2. I am currently reading an interesting [paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w14533) on the [Mundell-Fleming Trilemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impossible_trinity). Will be writing about it soon.
